Purity & Precision

Uncompromising Standard of Excellence

At Allentis Pharmaceuticals, quality is not a department; it is a company-wide culture. Our Quality Assurance (QA) and Quality Control (QC) protocols operate independently of commercial production, ensuring complete objectivity and uncompromising standards.

Our GLP-compliant testing laboratories are equipped with sophisticated analytical instruments including High-Performance Liquid Chromatographs (HPLC), Gas Chromatographs (GC), UV-Vis Spectrophotometers, FTIR, Atomic Absorption Spectrophotometers, and Dissolution Testers to verify identity, assay purity, and dissolution profiles for every single batch.

Methodical Compliance

Our 7-Step Quality Control Workflow

From raw API sampling to final market distribution, every phase undergoes strict verification.

Step 01
Raw Material Testing

Quarantine and comprehensive chemical testing of Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) and excipients prior to release into dispensing.

Step 02
In-Process Quality Checks (IPQC)

Continuous monitoring of weight variation, hardness, friability, disintegration time, and fill volume during active compression and filling.

Step 03
Microbiological Testing

Sterility testing, bacterial endotoxin analysis, and total viable aerobic microbial count in Class 100 laminar airflow workstations.

Step 04
Instrumental Assay & Impurities

Quantitative estimation of active drug content and related substances using calibrated High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC).

Step 05
Packaging & Leakage Integrity

Visual inspection, leak testing for blister/strip packs, and pinhole detection under automated camera inspection systems.

Step 06 & 07
COA Release & Retention

Final Certificate of Analysis (COA) issuance by Qualified Persons (QP) and secure archiving of control sample retains in stability chambers.
