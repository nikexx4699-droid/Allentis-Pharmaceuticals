Our Foundation

Driven by Science, Committed to Humanity

Incorporated over two decades ago, Allentis Pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd. has established itself as an ethical, innovation-led Indian pharmaceutical powerhouse. Our core objective is simple yet profound: to make advanced healthcare solutions accessible and affordable to every patient, regardless of geographic boundary.

From our humble beginnings with a single tablet formulation line, Allentis has expanded into a multi-divisional pharmaceutical entity manufacturing over 500 WHO-GMP compliant formulations spanning Antibiotics, Gastrointestinal, Cardiac, Anti-Diabetic, Respiratory, Pediatric, and Nutraceutical care.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as a premier, patient-centric pharmaceutical enterprise renowned for scientific innovation, unyielding quality standards, and ethical leadership.

Our Mission

To develop, manufacture, and market therapeutic products of high purity, safety, and efficacy, while empowering healthcare professionals and business partners worldwide.

Core Values

Integrity in scientific research, Uncompromising Quality Control, Patient-First Mindset, Continuous Innovation, and Sustainable Environmental Stewardship.

Our Milestones

Journey of Excellence

Key growth benchmarks in our path to global healthcare leadership.

2001
Company Incorporation

Allentis Pharmaceuticals was established with a dedicated solid dosage manufacturing unit in Gujarat, India.

2008
WHO-GMP & ISO Certification

Upgraded manufacturing infrastructure to WHO-GMP standards and expanded into liquid syrups and oral suspensions.

2015
Global Export Expansion

Initiated international export operations across Southeast Asia and Africa, launching over 150 registered dossiers.

2022
Advanced R&D & Sterile Injectables Unit

Commissioned state-of-the-art sterile injectable line and dedicated analytical testing lab for critical care formulations.

2026
Pan-India Franchise & 30+ Export Markets

Celebrating 500+ formulations and empowering over 12,000 PCD franchise and distribution partners worldwide.
