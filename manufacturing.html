Pristine Production Standards

WHO-GMP Certified Cleanroom Environment

Our manufacturing site spans over 150,000 sq. ft. of built-up area designed in strict adherence to WHO-GMP guidelines. The facility utilizes Class 10,000 (ISO Class 7) and Class 100 (ISO Class 5) cleanrooms equipped with terminal HEPA filtration, dedicated HVAC air handling units (AHU), positive differential pressure controls, and zero cross-contamination zoning.

Every production process—from raw material dispensing, high-shear granulation, high-speed rotary tablet compression, fluid bed drying, to automated blister packing—is computer-controlled to guarantee batch-to-batch consistency.

Engineering Superiority

Advanced Equipment & EHS Safety Standards

Modern machinery coupled with zero-emission Environment, Health & Safety protocols.

Automated High-Speed Machinery

High-capacity 55-station double rotary tablet presses, Auto-coater units, automatic capsule filling machines, and high-speed BFS (Blow-Fill-Seal) ampoule systems.

Environmental & HVAC Controls

Dedicated AHUs with 0.3-micron HEPA filtration maintaining strict temperature (22°C ± 2°C) and relative humidity (<40% RH) in moisture-sensitive formulation zones.

Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)

Comprehensive Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) and Reverse Osmosis (RO) purified water plants ensuring complete eco-friendly sustainable manufacturing.
