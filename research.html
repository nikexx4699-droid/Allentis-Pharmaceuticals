Pioneering Formulation Science

Transforming Complex Molecules into Patient-Friendly Therapies

At Allentis Pharmaceuticals, R&D is the engine driving our clinical innovation. Our state-of-the-art research wing houses over 40 experienced pharmaceutical scientists, analytical chemists, and regulatory specialists dedicated to advancing drug delivery systems, optimizing solubility, and enhancing bio-efficiency.

We focus on developing modified release (Sustained/Controlled Release), taste-masked pediatric suspensions, sublingual mouth-dissolving tablets, and stable fixed-dose combinations (FDCs) that maximize patient compliance.

Core Competencies

R&D Specializations

Rigorous analytical protocols and cutting-edge formulation techniques.

Novel Drug Delivery (NDDS)

Specializing in matrix tablets, gastro-retentive systems, and micro-pellet technology to deliver prolonged therapeutic plasma concentrations.

Analytical Method Validation

Developing robust, validated Stability Indicating Analytical Methods (SIAM) using Waters HPLC and Gas Chromatography (GC) systems.

Stability Studies (ICH Guidelines)

Accelerated and real-time stability testing in ICH-compliant environmental walk-in chambers covering Zone I through Zone IVB climatic conditions.
